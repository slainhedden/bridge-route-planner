Limits

Routes are for cars and ignore truck restrictions. The 2025 National Bridge Inventory provides mapped low-clearance records here. It misses rail-only overpasses and other obstacles. A flag means a bridge is near the route, not necessarily above your lane. No flags does not mean your vehicle will fit. Check current signs, lane clearance, and state DOT truck routing or permit guidance.
